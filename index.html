How to squint

  • Move: click or hold the mouse, or use WASD / arrow keys. Enemies body-block you.
  • Roll: Space. A quick dash that passes through enemies with brief immunity.
  • Sprint: hold Shift. Much faster, but one hit while sprinting stuns you for 2.5s.
  • Attack: automatic when an enemy is in range
  • Skills: Q, E, R. Three per class; the big one has the long cooldown.
  • Waypoints: touch a totem to unlock it (green entry, blue midpoint), press T to teleport
  • Dots: white normal, blue magic, yellow rare, orange boss
  • Goal: find the boss, dodge the telegraph circles, clear the map, level up
  • Death: costs 10% of your level XP, exactly as painful as it sounds
